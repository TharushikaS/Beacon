Beacon - AI-Powered Disaster Response and Recovery System

The system features the following key ideas and functionalities:

🔹 AI-Powered Flood & Landslide Prediction
👉 Analyzes rainfall, soil moisture, and past disaster data to provide early warnings for preventive actions.

🔹 Flood Segmentation & Depth Estimation
👉Uses DeepLabV3+ and GradCAM to map flooded areas from satellite & drone images, aiding rescue and relief operations.
🔹 Alternative Route Navigation
👉AI-driven rerouting based on flood depth, road closures, and vehicle suitability for safe evacuation.
🔹 AI-Powered Voice Alerts & Chatbot
👉Provides real-time spoken alerts and step-by-step safety guidance in Sinhala, Tamil, and English.
🔹 Post-Disaster Relief & Resource Coordination
👉Maps affected areas, coordinates aid distribution and connects people with shelters and emergency resources.
🔹 Offline Functionality & Smart Alerts
👉Operates offline with SMS-based alerts and offline maps, ensuring communication in low-connectivity areas.
🔹 Emergency Contact Database
👉 Connects users with emergency contacts and services based on location, offering quick access to help during crises.
